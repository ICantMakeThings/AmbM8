Chip is ESP32-P4 (revision v0.0)
Features: High-Performance MCU
Crystal is 40MHz
USB High-Speed is for audio interface, 192khz 4 channels in + 2 384khz channels out
and recording to SD

--- SD ---
GPIO 42 - D3
GPIO 44 - DI (CMD)
GPIO 43 - CLK
GPIO 39 - DO
GPIO 40 - D1 
GPIO 41 - D2


--- PCM5100A+HT97226 ---
GPIO 9 - SD
GPIO 6 - MC (possibly not needed)
GPIO 4 - BCK
GPIO 5 - DIN
GPIO 11 - WS 

--- ESP32-C3 (for wireless config) ---
Chip is ESP32-C3 (QFN32) (revision v0.4)
Features: WiFi, BLE, Embedded Flash 4MB (XMC)
Crystal is 40MHz
USB on C3 will be used as configuration via wire, or BLE.

GPIO 45 - UART4_TX (RX-ON-C3: GPIO20)
GPIO 46 - UART4_RX (TX-ON-C3: GPIO21)

--- PCM1865DBTR (Software-Controlled in Slave PLL Mode with 3.3v IOVDD) ---
GPIO 12 - DOUT
GPIO 14 - LRCK
GPIO 13 - BCK 
GPIO 22 - SDA
GPIO 23 - SCL
GPIO 15 - MC (Likely not needed as the PCM18xx is in slave mode, generating its own MC in PLL)
HAL ADDRESS: 0x94

--- I2C (0.96" OLED: SSD1315) ---
GPIO 22 - SDA
GPIO 23 - SCL
HAL ADDRESS: 0x78

--- SPI 1.54 Inch 200x200 Epaper Module ---
GPIO 16 - SDA 
GPIO 17 - SCL
GPIO 18 - CS
GPIO 19 - RES
GPIO 20 - BUSY
GPIO 21 - D/C

GPIO 29 - Side key 3 (Bottom - Left 1) REC/STOP CH
